Neutrality

What this site will and will not do, stated plainly enough to be held to.

Service time tracker · Duty days

Nobody pays for placement or influence

No agency, advisor, team, club, or media outlet pays for placement, influence, or modified output on this site. There is no paid placement, no referral fee, and no lead generation to advisors, agents, or agencies. Nothing here is sponsored, and no figure changes because of who is reading it.

One engine, one output

There is exactly one model. Everyone who loads a page sees the same numbers, computed by the same code, from the same public data. There is no agency version of the math, no adjustable weighting, and no early access to model changes.

Everything on this site is currently free and requires no account. If any of it is ever paid, a paid tier will buy convenience — exports, bulk views, saved work — and will never buy a different answer.

Built for the player, not against him

This project will not build a feature that models a club's leverage against a player, and it will not offer anyone a view of a player that the player cannot run about himself. Where those two goals conflict with a feature request, the feature loses.

It reports; it does not advise

The service-time figures are estimates reconstructed from public transaction records, and the duty-day tool is an organizational aid for a conversation with a qualified preparer. Neither is tax advice, financial advice, or a recommendation about any decision. The tools state what the numbers are and how they were reached; what to do about them is not theirs to say.

Every figure is meant to be checkable. The methodology, the known gaps, and the places the underlying data cannot answer are all documented rather than smoothed over — a number you cannot interrogate is not worth trusting.

What is not published

Nothing entered into the duty-day tool leaves the visitor's device. There is no account system, no server-side storage, and no analytics that could carry a salary, a domicile, or a contract term off the page. The site's only analytics are cookieless page counts.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by MLB or the MLBPA. Service time figures are estimates, not official MLB/MLBPA figures.
