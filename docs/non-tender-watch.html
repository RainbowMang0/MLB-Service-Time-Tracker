Non-tender watch for the 2027 season

Clubs must tender or release every arbitration-eligible player by the November 20, 2026 non-tender deadline. 84 players across 29 clubs meet this page's watch rule.

The rule: a player makes this list if he is arbitration-eligible and either already in his final arbitration year before free agency, or played in fewer than 20 games last season -- the two clearest service-time-and-playing-time signals this data can support. This is not a probability and not a prediction of what any club will do; it is the rule above, applied.
