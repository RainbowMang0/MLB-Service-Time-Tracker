Rookie eligibility

237 players on a 40-man roster still have rookie eligibility.

A player remains a rookie until previous seasons exceed 130 at-bats, 50 innings pitched, or 45 days on an active roster, not counting the injured list. September days count from 2020 on; before 2020 they do not. A 2020 mark means September 2020 is what pushes the active-day total over 45. Service time counts injured-list days. These figures are reconstructed from public data, not an official MLB status.
