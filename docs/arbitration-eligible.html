Arbitration-eligible players this winter (2027)

328 players across 30 clubs, by club and service time.

A player becomes eligible for salary arbitration once he reaches 3.000 years of major league service time, and stays eligible until he reaches free agency at 6.000. A smaller group reaches it a year early under the Super Two rule: players with 2.000-3.000 years of service who rank in the top 22% of that class, with at least 86 days of service in the preceding season. See the Super Two page for how that line is projected this year -- as a range, not one number.

The 328 players below are rostered players whose service time estimate meets one of those two tests today. Teams still choose whether to tender a contract to each of them; this page reports eligibility, not any club's decision. 29 of them already have a contract or extension on file (marked below), so an actual arbitration hearing is unlikely even though their service time qualifies. (6 of those are on the bubble -- within the projected range but short of its high end; see the Super Two page.)
