206 players on a 40-man roster are out of minor league options, and 281 have one option year left.

What an option year is

When a player is on the 40-man roster, his club can usually send him to the minors and call him back without waivers. Most players can be sent down in 3 separate seasons. Once those seasons are used, the club generally has to pass him through waivers before sending him down. These figures are our own counts from public data. A short stay in the minors does not always use a season. Some players qualify for an additional season because of injuries or a short early career. That depends on records this site does not have, so it is not guessed. A player whose public record starts after his career began is left off this list. The league keeps the official record.
