Questions

Which Chicago White Sox players are Rule 5 eligible in 2026?

Minor leaguers on a Chicago White Sox affiliate roster who are off the 40-man and whose first professional contract makes the December 2026 meeting one where they can be selected. The list is estimated from public signing data.

When does Chicago White Sox have to protect them?

November 17, 2026. The draft is scheduled for December 9, 2026. The labor agreement expires December 1, 2026, so that draft date may change.

Why are some players marked estimated or unknown?

Estimated means the earliest of more than one professional signing was used, or the player is a winter or spring international signee whose June 5 age date is unsettled. Unknown means a birth date or signing date is missing, or a fall signing falls where the public schedule does not settle whether that December meeting counts.
