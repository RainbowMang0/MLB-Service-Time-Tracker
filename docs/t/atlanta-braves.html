Rule 5

Rule 5 eligible Braves 2026 · league-wide

172 days credit a full year, so a season adds at most 1.000 no matter how long a player is on a roster. 3.000 years reaches salary arbitration and 6.000 reaches free agency — what every threshold unlocks. Players near a threshold are estimates.
