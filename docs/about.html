What this is

Big League Service Time Tracker estimates how much major league service time every player has built up, and tracks the milestones that time unlocks — salary arbitration and free agency. It is built from public data and our own calculations, not from any figures MLB or the players' association publish themselves.

How it stays current

The site updates every day, so a player's standing moves as his roster status changes. Because every figure here is an estimate reconstructed from the public record, it can differ from whatever official number MLB or a club may privately keep — those are not published anywhere we can check against.

What this isn't

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Major League Baseball, the Major League Baseball Players Association, or any club. Nothing here is financial, tax, or legal advice.
